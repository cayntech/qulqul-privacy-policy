# Privacy Policy — Qulqul Data

**App:** Qulqul Data (Qulqul Data Service)  
**Operator:** Qulqul Data Exchange  
**Effective date:** 20 September 2026

This page describes how the Android customer app handles information. It is
not legal advice.
Play Console needs a **public HTML URL**. The file to host is
[`privacy-policy.html`](privacy-policy.html) in this folder. A private GitHub
link will fail review.

## Who this app is for

Qulqul Data lets customers in Somalia browse mobile data bundles, create an
order, and pay through their mobile-money wallet. There is no account or
login. The app is not directed at children. Do not use it if you are under 13.

## Information we collect

- **Paying number** — entered in the app; stored on the device after a
  successful order.
- **Recipient number** — the phone that should receive the bundle.
- **Order details** — bundle, payment wallet, status, timestamps (on our
  servers, for dispatch and support).
- **Offline SMS** — if there is no internet, the app opens the system SMS
  composer with a pre-filled order. We do not read the SMS inbox.

We do not collect names, emails, passwords, precise location, contacts,
photos, wallet PINs, USSD secrets, card numbers, or ad identifiers. The app
has no ads.

## How we use it

To create and track the order, match payment to the bundle and recipient,
deliver the package, and handle failed orders. We do not sell personal
information or use it for ads.

## Where it is stored

- **On the phone:** paying number in app storage. Uninstalling deletes it.
- **On our servers:** order fields needed to fulfill and support the order.

## Who we share it with

Hosting/database for the API; mobile-network operators as needed to send the
ordered bundle; the user’s SMS and dialer apps when they confirm those
actions. We may disclose information if required by law or to prevent abuse.

## Permissions

Internet for catalog and orders. No SMS, contacts, or location permission.
Payment opens the system dialer. Offline orders open the system SMS composer.

## Retention and deletion

Orders are kept as needed for fulfillment, support, and the business ledger.
To request deletion of server data for a number, use in-app Contact.
